# Build a Complete Arabic RTL Order, Inventory, Delivery & Financial Management Web App

I want you to build a complete production-ready web application for managing a small personal/group business that purchases products from SHEIN on behalf of customers.

The application must be Arabic-first, fully RTL, mobile-first, responsive, modern, simple, fast, and easy to use from both mobile phones and desktop computers.

Two business partners will use the application from different phones at the same time. Therefore, ALL data must be stored centrally in a persistent server-side database. Do NOT store business data only in browser localStorage or device storage.

The application must support real-time/shared data access so that if one partner adds or updates an order, payment, expense, shipment, or product, the other partner can see the same updated data.

---

# 1. Core Business Workflow

The actual business workflow is:

1. A customer contacts us and requests one or more products.
2. We tell the customer the selling price for each product, including our commission.
3. The customer pays approximately 50% upfront.
4. We purchase the products from SHEIN.
5. The actual SHEIN purchase price is usually lower than the price charged to the customer.
6. One SHEIN invoice/order can contain products belonging to multiple different customers.
7. Multiple SHEIN invoices can later be combined into one shipment from the shipping company.
8. The shipment eventually arrives in Gaza.
9. We sort the products according to customer and product image/details.
10. Before delivering products, we collect the remaining amount for the products that have actually arrived.
11. A customer may have agreed from the beginning to receive the order by delivery.
12. Some products may arrive while other products belonging to the same customer/order have not arrived yet.
13. We may choose to keep arrived products undelivered until the remaining product(s) arrive.
14. Therefore, product status and order status must be separate concepts.
15. Financial transactions must be tracked separately from order/product status.

# 2. Important Financial Principle

DO NOT confuse:

* Customer selling price
* SHEIN actual purchase price
* Commission
* Customer payments
* Business expenses
* Current wallet balance
* Business profit

These must be separate fields and calculations.

The current wallet balance is NOT the same as profit.

For example, money currently in the wallet may include customer deposits for orders that have not yet been completed.

# 16. Important Wallet Rule

When a customer payment is recorded:

→ wallet balance increases.

When SHEIN is paid:

→ wallet balance decreases.

When transportation is paid:

→ wallet balance decreases.

When shipping is paid:

→ wallet balance decreases.

When another business expense is paid:

→ wallet balance decreases.

Do NOT allow the dashboard balance to be manually typed.

The balance must always be calculated from the transaction ledger.

# 29. Data Integrity

This is critical.

Use proper relational database design.

Do NOT duplicate financial totals unnecessarily if they can be calculated from source transactions.

Use foreign keys and relationships.

Use database transactions for operations that affect multiple records.

Example:

When recording a customer payment:

1. Create payment record.
2. Create wallet income transaction.
3. Update relevant balance/state.

These should succeed or fail together.

Similarly, when recording a SHEIN purchase:

1. Create purchase record.
2. Link products.
3. Create wallet expense transaction.

Do not allow inconsistent partial data.
